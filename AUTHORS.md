## Authorship Declaration
This repository presents a wholly fictional, deterministic academic digital forensics simulation titled **Operation Hydra** targeting a fictional organization (**Asterion Financial Services Pvt. Ltd.**).

- All email samples, headers, IP addresses (RFC 5737 TEST-NET ranges), domains (`.example` / `.invalid` RFC 2606 reserved suffixes), endpoint telemetry events, authentication logs, bank accounts, UPI/IMPS/NEFT references, and cryptocurrency wallet identifiers are synthetic.
- No live threat infrastructure was probed, no third-party APIs are required to execute the analysis, and no real-world victims, credentials, or financial systems are involved.
- Legal citations reference publicly available statutory texts (Information Technology Act, 2000; Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023; CERT-In Directions, 2022; 18 U.S.C. § 1030; EU GDPR; Council of Europe Budapest Convention) and are provided strictly for academic classification rather than legal advice.
