# References

## Indian Statutory and Regulatory Sources

1. **Information Technology Act, 2000** (Act No. 21 of 2000) — Sections 43, 66, 66C, 66D, 70B. Official repository: India Code, Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1999
2. **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)** — Cyber Laws & Information Technology Act Overview — https://www.meity.gov.in/content/cyber-laws
3. **CERT-In Directions under Section 70B(6) of the IT Act, 2000** (No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In, dated 28 April 2022) — 6-hour incident reporting, 180-day log retention, and NIC/NPL NTP synchronization mandates — https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf
4. **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)** (Act No. 45 of 2023, effective 1 July 2024) — Sections 61 (Criminal Conspiracy), 318 (Cheating), 319 (Cheating by Personation), 336 (Forgery / False Electronic Record), 340 (Forged Electronic Record Used as Genuine). Official repository: India Code — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
5. **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)** (Act No. 47 of 2023) — Section 63 (Admissibility of Electronic Records, replacing Indian Evidence Act § 65B) — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
6. **Supreme Court of India — *Shreya Singhal v. Union of India*, (2015) 5 SCC 1** (striking down Section 66A of the IT Act, 2000).
